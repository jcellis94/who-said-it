        How to play

        1. Everyone joins with the game code. Every phone shows the same round, answers and scores.
        2. Pick a reader. Everyone, the reader included, secretly writes an answer on paper and hands it in.
        3. The reader reads every answer aloud. The player on the reader's left guesses first by picking an answer and naming who wrote it.
        4. A right guess scores 1 point, puts that writer out for the round, and earns another guess. A wrong guess passes the turn to the left.
        5. The round ends when one writer is left unguessed. That player scores 2 points.
        6. The player on the reader's left reads next. Play until everyone has been reader. The top score wins.
